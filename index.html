Our Story

Avira. Perfume from the harvest, made by hand in Odisha.

Our heritage

India has been making fragrance for centuries, from attars to flowers, woods and resins. Avira carries that tradition forward in a form that’s modern and affordable, so the scent of home is available to everyone.

From the harvest

Our scents follow the seasons. Each season we work with natural oils from different farmers from India so our range changes with what the land gives us. Four to five signature scents, never quite the same year after year.

Made by hand

Every bottle is made by hand—formulated, blended, bottled and packed by hand in Bhubaneswar. No factory line, no shortcuts, just people who care about every batch.

What’s inside

Natural oils and perfumer’s alcohol. No synthetic fragrance ingredients. Our perfumes are certified and last more than eight hours, depending on the climate.

The bottle and the box

Your Avira arrives in recycled cardboard, wrapped in up-cycled satin cloth, with a pressurised atomiser for a fine, even mist. Packaging that feels like a gift, without the waste.

A circular journey

From the farm to your hands, we try to waste nothing. Natural ingredients from growers, recycled packaging, reused fabric. Every rupee we save by making it ourselves goes back into better ingredients, not advertising.

Our promise

Honest ingredients. Handmade care. Fair price. Avira is premium perfume at ₹450 that you can trust, because you can see exactly how it’s made.

How To Use
Our Product

Spray about 3 times on the same spot for good projection.

Spray it from a distance of 10cm away from the body.

Apply a natural lotion at the pulse point for longevity.

Don't rub the perfume after applying.

Keep inside refrigerator for 5 days for maturation.

Prefer cotton clothes for greater experience.
